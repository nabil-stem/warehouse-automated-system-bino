THE APPROACH

Complex operations.
Visible decisions.

A robot mission is more than a route. It is a sequence of decisions about space, traffic, lifting, and the next task.

Explore the whole system
SPACE

Build the warehouse around the mission.

Adjust rack rows, aisles, shelf levels, inventory, and station positions. See how a different layout changes the world your robots navigate.

MOVEMENT

Watch a fleet find its rhythm.

Follow A* routes, traffic reservations, yielding, and station queues. Switch from an overview to a robot camera or a close-up of the scissor lift.

INSIGHT

Understand what happens between tasks.

Inspect mission phases, waiting, congestion, throughput, and physical collision counts. Export scenarios and traces to continue your analysis.
